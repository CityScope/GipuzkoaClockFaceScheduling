Why symmetry, not just coordination

Timing one change at one station is easy. A network of timed hubs only works when every line repeats with the same rhythm, the hub minutes are symmetric, and the travel times between hubs fit the clock.

Rule of thumb from the Bavarian ITF study (Hesse et al., p. 6): main hubs at :00 or :30 and edge times of n · T/2 − D minutes (T = 60 or 120, D ≈ 3–10), e.g. 27, 56 or 85 minutes. See the literature notes.
